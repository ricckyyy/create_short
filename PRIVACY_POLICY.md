# Privacy Policy for AI Content Studio

**Effective Date:** November 13, 2025

## Introduction

AI Content Studio ("we," "our," or "us") is an automated video creation tool that helps content creators generate short-form videos for social media platforms.

## Information We Collect

### TikTok User Data
When you authorize our application, we may access:
- TikTok user ID
- Basic profile information (username, display name)
- Video upload permissions

### Usage Data
- Video generation history
- Script content created through the application
- API usage logs

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- Generate and upload videos to your TikTok account
- Improve our video generation algorithms
- Maintain and optimize our services

## Data Storage

- All data is stored locally on your device
- No personal information is shared with third parties
- TikTok access tokens are stored securely using industry-standard encryption

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Revoke access to your TikTok account at any time
- Request deletion of your data
- Access your stored information

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **TikTok API**: For video upload and publishing
- **Pexels API**: For stock video footage
- **VOICEVOX**: For text-to-speech synthesis (local)

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
